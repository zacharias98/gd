Founder
The Founder

Giorgos Damianakis

Signature Hospitality Management

My name is Giorgos Damianakis. My connection with entrepreneurship and creation began at a very young age — through production units and family businesses in the food sector.

I studied Food Technology at the Agricultural University of Athens. Yet it was tourism — not food science alone — that truly captured me.

Engaging with hospitality at a young age showed me how deeply I love creating experiences, developing hospitality concepts, and connecting with people from around the world.

From food science to guest experiences

That passion led me to continue my studies in Business Administration, completing a Master's in Hospitality and Tourism at the Hellenic Mediterranean University of Crete.

Building projects, building expertise

Through creating and developing my own projects — Volta Suites & Villas, Volta Seaside Boutique Hotel, and Rodo Seafront Villa — I gained hands-on experience across every layer of boutique hospitality.

That journey shaped how I think about properties: not as assets alone, but as living brands with a soul, a rhythm, and a guest journey worth refining.

Why GD Signature exists

GD Signature Hospitality Management was born from a simple desire: to share this knowledge and experience with fellow owners who want to evolve their property in a meaningful way — and benefit from it — without dedicating endless hours to the operational weight themselves.

We help owners elevate their properties with strategy, soul, and operational clarity — so hospitality feels intentional again, not overwhelming.
